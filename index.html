How We Can Help

🔎 Understand the Fraud

Learn what to do after UPI fraud, phishing, fake job offers or suspicious transactions.

🏦 Contact Your Bank

Report unauthorized transactions to your bank immediately and follow its official instructions.

📋 File an Official Complaint

Keep transaction IDs, screenshots, phone numbers and other evidence ready for your complaint.

🔐 Secure Your Accounts

Change compromised passwords, secure your email and never share OTPs, PINs or recovery codes.

Important Safety Notice

Never pay an unknown person who guarantees recovery of your lost money. Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, bank password or remote-access permissions with an unverified recovery agent.

No recovery outcome can be guaranteed. Use official reporting channels and verified professional services only.
